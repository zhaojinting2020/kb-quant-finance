## Quant Funds Use a Scientific Approach…
Why not You?
![Image 36: 📊](https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/svg/1f4ca.svg)

<p class="kb-image-caption">图例</p>

** At one point, I came across a research paper from the department of Computer Science,**University College London** (based on the work of **Marco Lopez de Prado**) that highlighted the **importance of a scientific process** in selecting trading strategies.

The **father of quantitative finance, Jim Simons** (who achieved **66% annual returns for nearly 40 years** with **Renaissance Technologies**) said:
_“Our scheme is to analyze data and markets to test for statistical significance and consistency over time.”_**Jim Simon**
![Image 37: ⬇️](https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/svg/2b07.svg)

<p class="kb-image-caption">图例</p>

As independent traders, we often operate with smaller amounts of capital. At first glance, **you might think that’s a disadvantage**.

But actually… **it’s an edge!** We don’t have to worry about moving the markets and cannibalizing our own profits.
![Image 48: 🚨](https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/svg/1f6a8.svg)

<p class="kb-image-caption">图例</p>

When large funds manage huge sums, they often have to **split their entries into multiple orders** to avoid pushing prices too much. But even then, their trades can move the market, causing them to enter positions later than planned, **eating into their profits**.
**Smaller accounts** (even $100,000 to $200,000 or more) don’t have this issue, as **Jim Simons himself has pointed out**.

On top of that, having a **clear and simplified scientific process** allows me to create new strategies **with minimal time investment**.

You only need about 5 hours a week to build your strategies—often even less. (I’ll break that down for you in just a moment.) But the ultimate benefit you probably don’t expect? **Peace of mind.** You know you have a system. You focus on improving it, **but you no longer have to spend hours staring at charts, stressing in front of your screens**.
_The only system you need_
